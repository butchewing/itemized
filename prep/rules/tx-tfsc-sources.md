## The Texas General Price List rule: 22 TAC §203.46

TFSC's §203.46 restates the federal General Price List rule (16 CFR 453.2) for Texas, with additions. Its subsection (b)(5) is the Texas GPL:

- (A)(iii) the list must show the provider's name, address and telephone number, the caption "general price list", and the effective date.
- (B) retail prices, "if offered for sale", for forwarding and receiving remains, direct cremation and immediate burial (each with the federal sub-items), transfer of remains, embalming, other preparation, facilities and staff for viewing, funeral ceremony and memorial service, equipment and staff for graveside service, hearse, limousine, and **(xiv) "filing a claim seeking life insurance proceeds on behalf of the beneficiaries."**
- (C)(i)-(ii) caskets and outer burial containers: **either** a price range "together with the statement: 'A complete price list will be provided at the funeral home.'", **or** individual prices.
- (C)(iii) the basic services fee and its statement, as federally; "and overhead" may be added.
- **(C)(iv) "If the funeral home charges for processing the insurance claim, that fee shall be disclosed."**
- **(C)(v)** when a consumer means to pay with insurance proceeds, the provider requires payment before the proceeds arrive, and the provider does not file the claim (or the consumer does not use its service), the GPL must include: "Please note that if you utilize a third party to file a claim seeking expedited receipt of life insurance proceeds on behalf of a beneficiary, there will be a fee to be paid associated with the filing of such a claim."
- (b)(4) **urn price list**: retail prices of urns not needing special order, the predominant material (bronze as sheet or cast bronze), the effective date, and the provider's name, address, phone and the caption "urn price list". Not needed if the GPL carries the same information.
- (b)(2)-(3) casket and outer burial container price lists, as federally, with Texas wording for the required specifications.

## Other TFSC rules on price-list content

- **§203.48 Misrepresentations** restates 16 CFR 453.3: the embalming disclosure ("Except in certain special cases, embalming is not required by law..."), the alternative-container disclosure next to direct cremation prices, the outer burial container disclosure ("In most areas of the country..."), preservative and protective claims, and cash advances. The wording matches the federal disclosures Itemized already checks; confirm word for word when building.
- **§203.49 Required purchases**: the selection notice "immediately above the prices", worded as federally: the basic-services sentence only "if the charge for 'services of funeral director and staff' cannot be declined", and "and overhead" optional.
- **§203.51**: disclosures clear and conspicuous; nothing on the price lists that alters or contradicts them (as 16 CFR 453.7).

## The statute: Occupations Code §651.405 and §651.4055

- §651.405(a): the funeral establishment's retail price list must include the retail price of transfer, embalming, facility for viewing, facility for funeral services, hearse, limousine, caskets, outer enclosures, **(9) filing a claim seeking life insurance proceeds on behalf of the beneficiaries**, and other itemized staff services.
- §651.405(b): name, address, telephone, effective date, and the selection notice **as fixed text, including "However, any funeral arrangements you select will include a charge for our basic services and overhead."**
- §651.4055: a crematory's retail price list: name, address, telephone, effective date, and a notice **without** the basic-services sentence, ending "describing the goods and services you selected". The commission may require more by rule; Ch. 205's rule titles include none on price lists.

## How Texas differs from the federal checks Itemized runs (ftc-453@2026.1)

| Topic | Federal | Texas | For Itemized |
|---|---|---|---|
| Life-insurance claim filing | Not required | Price, if offered: §651.405(a)(9), §203.46(b)(5)(B)(xiv) | New check |
| Insurance-claim processing fee | Not required | Disclosed if charged: §203.46(b)(5)(C)(iv) | New check, combined with the one above |
| Third-party insurance statement | Not required | Required in some circumstances: §203.46(b)(5)(C)(v) | Conditions depend on facts not on the list: review at most |
| Urns | No urn list | Urn prices with material, or an urn price list: §203.46(b)(4) | Possible check when urns are listed on the GPL |
| Selection notice | Basic-services sentence conditional; "and overhead" optional | Statute: fixed, with "and overhead" (§651.405(b)); TFSC rule: as federal (§203.49) | Statute and rule disagree: review, not fail |
| Crematory notice | None | Shorter notice: §651.4055(c) | When the list says the provider operates a crematory: review if missing |
| Casket, outer container ranges | Range plus statement allowed | Range plus "A complete price list will be provided at the funeral home." allowed: §203.46(b)(5)(C) | Federal checks already cover it |
| Name, address, phone, date, caption, other items | Covered | Same | Covered |

## Decisions (Butch, 2026-11-26)

1. **Caskets and outer enclosures:** first decided as the most explicit reading (range-only as "needs review"). **Dropped** once §203.46(b)(5)(C) was read: TFSC's rule expressly allows a range with "A complete price list will be provided at the funeral home." No Texas casket check; the federal checks cover ranges.
2. **Items not offered:** only items the funeral establishment offers must be priced. §203.46(b)(5)(B) agrees ("if offered for sale").
3. **Combined funeral establishment and crematory:** both lists' requirements apply, so both notices are expected.

4. **First version of the Texas checks:** insurance claim filing (price if offered; processing fee disclosed), selection notice wording (statute vs TFSC rule: review), crematory notice (review), third-party insurance statement (review) and urn prices with material (review).

## Duties that are not about the list's content (not checkable from a document)

- Consumer brochure: provide it when services are discussed (§651.404), at the start of arrangements (§651.454(1), §203.26), display it (§201.7(c)).
- Give the price list to anyone inquiring in person; no contract before it is provided; price information by telephone (§651.454(2)-(4), §203.46(b)(1), (5)(A)).
- Keep price lists for two years (§651.460(a)(2)).
- Display at least two full-size adult caskets, one the lowest priced (§203.25).
- Website advertising: licensed name and license number on the contact page; no false or misleading advertising (§203.11).
- Purchase agreements (§651.406, §651.4065, §203.47): a separate document, for later.
